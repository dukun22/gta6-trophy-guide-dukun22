    Missables & warnings

    Before you start

    • No mission replay, no New Game+. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter — the game has 15 manual save slots. (PowerPyx)
    • Cheats disable trophies and saving. Keep them off for the whole run, and note that cheats only unlock after the story. (PowerPyx)
    • Everything else can be done after the story. Free-roam stays open after the credits, all quest givers return, and nothing on the 100% checklist is missable. (PowerPyx)
    • Four trophies are still unconfirmed at launch. Their triggers are marked “unconfirmed” in their guides; check PowerPyx’s guide for updates as players confirm them.
